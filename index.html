Parent Newsletter — 2026–27 Season

Welcome to the Season!

We're excited to kick things off with some big news: the club is at its largest enrollment since 2013, with a record 8 Mite teams and 3 Squirt teams. Thank you for being part of it!

Club News

  • New video system: We've upgraded to Hudl at KSU, replacing LiveBarn. Home games will be recorded and live streamed, and away games can be streamed too.
  • House hockey is growing: Sessions now run both Saturdays and Sundays, starting August 16. Goalie training will run on the other half of the ice.
  • New sound system at KSU — the speaker issues are fixed.
  • Golf outing success: Our first-year golf outing raised $7,000, with about $5,000 going toward Night at the Races.

Mark Your Calendars

All club events — and every game once schedules are released — live on the Schedule tab, where you can also grab a volunteer spot.

Night at the Races (Nov 6): Each team will assemble a themed basket. Interested in sponsoring? A new Diamond Sponsor tier ($2,500) includes a table of eight, race tickets, ads, and four golf outing spots.

Gear & Equipment

  • Neck guards are required for 12U, 14U, and high school, and recommended for 8U and 10U.
  • Mouth guards are required for 12U and up — attached to the helmet or worn in the mouth.
  • Practice jerseys: Please return last year's jerseys, clean, during the first week of practice.
  • Borrowing gear? Use the new online checkout form — QR codes are posted at gear locations.
  • Parking passes are available for pickup at the rink now.
  • A small charge (~$20–30) for summer ice and July 4th parade costs will appear on season bills.

Conduct & Policies

  • Parent Code of Conduct: Every family will review and sign the code of conduct at their team's parent meeting early in the season. One parent per family must attend.
  • The 24-Hour Rule: Please wait at least 24 hours after a game before contacting a coach with any concerns.
  • In the stands: No negative comments toward referees, opposing teams, or players, and no entering the player bench. Violations can result in suspensions of 14–30 days from Mid-Am. An ejection from a game results in a home game suspension.
  • Cell phones in locker rooms: Strict zero cell phone policy. Phones seen out will be held and returned to parents.
  • Online behavior: Cyberbullying via social media is a CSHL and USA Hockey Safe Sport violation. Please talk with your player about this.
  • Reporting: For injuries, conduct concerns, or other issues, use the incident form on the club website under Team Resources.

We Need Volunteers!

  • Game-day helpers: penalty box for every game; scoreboard and music for home games. Sign up right on the Schedule tab!
  • Locker room monitors — especially for 12U and 14U. Parents must complete free Safe Sport training first.
  • 50/50 raffle sellers for Monsters opening night in October (~3,000 tickets to sell!).
  • Night at the Races coordinator — Carol is stepping down after years of service, and we're looking for someone to take over this rewarding role.
Questions? Reach out to your team manager. See you at the rink! 🏒
