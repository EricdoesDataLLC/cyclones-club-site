Conduct & Policies

A few important reminders as we start the season:

  • Parent Code of Conduct: Every family will review and sign the code of conduct at their team's parent meeting early in the season. One parent per family must attend.
  • The 24-Hour Rule: Please wait at least 24 hours after a game before contacting a coach with any concerns.
  • In the stands: No negative comments toward referees, opposing teams, or players, and no entering the player bench. Violations can result in suspensions of 14–30 days from the Mid-Am governing body. An ejection from a game results in a home game suspension.
  • Cell phones in locker rooms: The Cyclones have a strict zero cell phone policy in locker rooms. Phones seen out will be held and returned to parents.
  • Online behavior: Cyberbullying via social media is a CSHL and USA Hockey Safe Sport violation. Please talk with your player about this.
  • Reporting: For injuries, conduct concerns, or other issues, use the incident form on the club website under Team Resources — and also speak with a coach or the team manager.
Questions? Reach out to your team manager. See you at the rink! 🏒
